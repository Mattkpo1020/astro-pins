Astro Pins 0.17.1 uses Godot 4.5.1 (MIT). Godot license and copyright notices are retained alongside this file. The exported loader streams the unchanged WASM from checked chunks and avoids cloning the response for unloadAfterInit. Backing canvas uses DPR up to 2 with a 1.6 megapixel budget. No threads, no native vibration emulation, and an optional no-audio startup. Game saves use an app-specific /userfs/AstroPinsAlpha persistent mount. No player data, server-side functions or Sites dependency are included.
